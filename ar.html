Storage cabinet: see it in your room

Three options, built to their listed sizes. Open one on your iPhone and it appears on your floor at true scale.

AR needs Safari on an iPhone or iPad. On this device, tapping a cabinet downloads its USDZ model instead.

  1. Tap a cabinet. The camera opens.
  2. Move your phone slowly over the floor until the cabinet appears. It's locked at 100% size.
  3. Drag it against the wall by the slider and twist with two fingers to turn it. Its back is the side facing away from you when it appears.
  4. Tap the shutter to save a photo, then try the next one.
Models come from the same code as the 3D apartment (src/furniture/cabinets/); regenerate with pnpm run export:ar. Sizes are body sizes; knobs and pulls stick out about 1″ more.
